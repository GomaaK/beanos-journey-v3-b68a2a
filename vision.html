Four campaigns

Invite past guests back to see what's new. “Remember your first Beano's?” and a free mini cake when they return. Goal: 10% more visits in 8 weeks.

The Morning Club · from Day 31

Reward people who start their day with us. Join through the Wi-Fi page or app; every 8th breakfast is free. Goal: 15% more orders before 11 am.

Signature Collection · every season

Drinks you can only get at Beano's: a new iced or wellness drink each season. Goal: 12% of drink sales.

Still Brewing, since 2000 · always on

Tell our story since the first branch in Zamalek: regulars' stories, the Triple Layer Cheesecake and a memories contest. Goal: stories shared 2× more.

Marketing and growth view: trends and what they mean for us

TikTok first for Gen Z

TikTok reaches about 75.7% of Egyptian adults and grew 18% in a year; Instagram about 28.5%. Instagram stays for heritage and regulars. S10

Use the bean-price relief

Arabica is about 17% cheaper than a year ago on the World Bank figure, and 34% cheaper on the 9 November market price, with further easing expected. Egypt's own cost of landing the beans must still be confirmed. Reinvest in the cup, and keep a price-watch line for El Niño. S11S12

Protect frequency under inflation

Egypt's inflation is 12% and cafés are among the services pushing prices. Refill bundles and value offers keep visits steady. Keep prices within about 10% of Cilantro, a main competitor with 82 outlets in 2023. S8S13S14

Cold, iced and customised

Gen Z leads iced and customised drinks all day, and matcha and wellness drinks are rising. Global evidence, mostly vendor-sourced. S15S17S18

The café as a place to belong

Industry voices see community and belonging at the centre of cafés. It supports the living-room idea. S17S19

Specialty is still small

About 300 to 400 million EGP of a 5 billion EGP coffee market. Our biggest growth is the mass-market guest, not only specialty fans. S9
