The growth engine

Wi-Fi sign-in, the app and our data.

Owning the relationship with our guests is the biggest lever in the plan. We turn the Wi-Fi password into the first step, and we make the most of the data Beano's already holds.

The idea in one picture

1The guest connectsWi-Fi is the reason to sign in.
2We know the guestA consented phone number and, soon, the app.
3Right offer, right momentMorning Club, Work Bundle, win-back.
4The guest returnsMore visits and a bigger check.
↺ Every visit teaches us more, so the next offer is better

Guests get the Wi-Fi password by signing in through the Beano's app. Every guest who stays for hours becomes a known guest: an installed app, a consented phone number and a reason to come back. It gives us what the delivery apps keep to themselves. F1S8

Why it fits Beano's

HoursGuests already stay for hours: laptop workers at U Venues, 90th Street, Makram Ebeid and Zamalek, and families at District 5.F1

Three stages, so the guest never feels friction

1

Wi-Fi sign-in page

No install. Phone number plus a clear opt-in. Pilot at 90th Street and Makram Ebeid (week 6 of the 90-day plan).

Measure: sign-ins as a share of Wi-Fi sessions; opt-in rate (proposed 30% by month 3)
2

The Beano's app is the faster way in

One tap to the Wi-Fi, an instant reward such as a first-drink treat, and the Morning Club (8th breakfast free). Guests who prefer not to install can still sign in on the web page.

Measure: install rate, second visit within 14 days, repeat rate
3

App-first Wi-Fi, where it works

Only in the work-heavy branches, and only after stages 1 and 2 prove conversion without hurting reviews. Tourists and one-time guests keep a simple path.

Decision gate: Q2 2027, from the pilot data

Why stages: an app install before Wi-Fi adds friction, and a guest who cannot connect fast posts a bad review. Egypt-specific evidence for app-gated Wi-Fi is thin, so this is a pilot with clear exit numbers, not a promise. ZUS Coffee in Malaysia reports about 70% of sales through its app S18, but it is a different market and a vendor source.

The legal guardrail we build in from day one
