The gaps

Nine gaps. Where we are, where we want to be.

Each gap shows what we saw today, the target, the first move, who owns it and how we will measure it. Four are fixed now; five are planned.

G1

Coffee quality and consistency

Fix now
Today

Cortado 2.5, 3.5, 4, 4, 4 out of 5; none reached 5.

Where we want to be

4/5 or better in every branch, served in 5 minutes or less.

First move

Recalibrate grind and extraction; a weekly taste check; then review beans and roast. Bean prices are easing globally, but Egyptian landed cost has to be confirmed with purchasing.

OwnerKitchen and purchasing
Measure

4/5 or better; drink wait 5 min or less (proposed)

G2

Service speed and accuracy

Fix now
Today

Drink waits of 3 to 13 minutes; forgotten ashtray, coconut water and drink; fixes taking from 1 to 33 minutes.

Where we want to be

Drinks first, an order check when food lands, and any problem solved in 15 minutes or less.

First move

Drinks-first rule; order check; apologise, replace, manager visits.

OwnerBranch managers
Measure

100% of branches; resolved in 15 min or less (proposed)

G3

Product knowledge and upselling

Fix now
Today

“Real turkey” was cold cuts; “barbecue sauce” was paprika and jalapeño; one thermal-cup sale in six visits.

Where we want to be

Every guest hears an accurate answer and one good suggestion.

First move

Product facts card; an upsell line; learn from the District 5 manager; monthly refreshers.

OwnerTraining lead
Measure

100% accurate answers; upsell offered at 80% of tables by Day 60 (proposed)

G4

Loyalty, the app and guest data

Plan
Today

No loyalty or app at any branch we visited. Guest data sits with the delivery apps.

Where we want to be

Know our regulars by name and reach them directly, with consent.

First move

Wi-Fi sign-in page, then the Beano's app as the faster, rewarded way in, then the Morning Club. See the growth engine.

OwnerMarketing and IT
Measure

30% opt-in by Month 3; members at 15% of weekly guests by Day 90 (proposed)

G5

Brand and retail consistency

Plan
Today

Four pack styles; generic sachets; no sign inside U Venues; several taglines; retail shelf below eye level; merch in a closed glass cabinet.

Where we want to be

One look, one voice, one packaging system.

First move

Signs above every counter; branded condiments; an eye-level retail wall; one slogan.

OwnerBrand manager and operations
Measure

Every touchpoint on one system by launch; retail 5% of sales by 2028 (proposed)

G6

Menu focus and a hero

Plan
Today

About 150 dine-in items and no dish or drink guests ask for by name.

Where we want to be

A clear hero dish and a hero drink.

First move

Trim to 100 to 110 items keeping 95% of revenue; build the Signature Collection around iced and cold drinks.

OwnerKitchen and marketing
Measure

Signature share of drink sales 12% or more (proposed)

Audience: kids, Gen Z and all-day breakfast

Plan
Today

No kids' menu; all-day breakfast never mentioned; Gen Z drifting to specialty cafés.

Where we want to be

A reason for every guest type to choose Beano's.

First move

Kids' mini menu at District 5 first; a breakfast table card; iced, matcha and wellness drops.

18 to 24 share up 5 points in 12 months (proposed)

G9

Data we already hold is not classified

Plan
Today

POS, delivery apps, the hotline, reviews, Instagram and the 2017 app all hold guest and sales data. We have not seen it used or joined up.

Where we want to be

One view of who comes, when, what they buy and who has stopped coming.

First move

A 30-day data audit; classify guests and products; build one dashboard; feed the segments to the Morning Club and campaigns.

OwnerBrand manager, IT and finance
Measure

Every measure has a starting number by day 30 (proposed)
